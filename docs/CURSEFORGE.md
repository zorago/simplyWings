## Description (anglais)

**Simply Wings** adds 41 pairs of collectible wings to Minecraft, each with its own look, animation, sounds, stats and personality, and a flight system made for them.

### Two ways to fly
- **Hover** to build: steady, precise, with a tiny wing beat.
- **Travel flight** to explore: beat your wings for speed, glide to recover stamina, dive, brake, and steer with the keys while the mouse orbits the camera.

Every flight action is a key of its own in the Controls menu.

### Collect them all
Each pair has stats (endurance, acceleration, speed, handling, stability, glide, lift), and every pair you own adds a small bonus to all the others. From common leather wings to the legendary Angel and Demon, and the mythic **Night** and **Light** with their tails, shrieks and invisible dash.

### Earn them in survival
- Imbue your tools with **winged energy** and gather 59 special drops from mobs, blocks and fishing.
- Harvest **essences** by flying through clouds, over the Nether's lava and the End's void.
- Craft components at the **Forge Bench**, assemble pairs at the **Assembly Table**, compress essences in the **Cloud Compressor**.
- Each dragon death raises a new **sanctum** in the End sky.

### Or summon them
Burn **summoning feathers** at the **Draconic Altar** and watch the summoning show unfold in the sky, seen by every player around. Five tiers, pity counters, and an **Offering Fountain** that turns your duplicates back into feathers.

### Everything explained in game
A full in-game **wiki** (270 pages) and an optional **JEI** integration.

### Compatibility
No dependency besides NeoForge, no mixin, no access transformer. Built and tested for a 195-mod pack (Iris, Sodium, Distant Horizons, Create Aeronautics…).
